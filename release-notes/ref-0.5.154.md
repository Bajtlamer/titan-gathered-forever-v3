# Titan Gathered Forever 0.5.154

Update from 0.5.151. This text is the changelog for the website and the release page. Versions 0.5.152 and 0.5.153 were internal steps and are included here. The core has one new file (WeaponMasters.lua), so fully restart the game once after updating; a plain reload is not enough.

## Added

- **Weapon masters on the map.** The world map and the minimap show the weapon masters of your faction in the capitals: Woo Ping (Stormwind), Buliwyf Stonehand and Bixi Wobblebonk (Ironforge), Ilyenia Moonfire (Darnassus) for the Alliance, and Hanashi and Sayoc (Orgrimmar), Ansekhwa (Thunder Bluff) and Archibald (Undercity) for the Horde. The tooltip of a pin lists the weapon skills the master teaches and shows at a glance which ones your character already knows (the weapon and the word Known in green), which it can still learn (white), which its class cannot use and which need level 20 (polearms), both in gray. A price is shown only when the game reported it in the window of that trainer; with no known price nothing is written. The pins can be switched off in the Titan menu (Trainers and recipe vendors, Show weapon masters) or in the map menu.
- **Map options.** The button on the world map holds every display setting of the map as a switch: gathering spots on the world map and the minimap and per profession, profession trainers, recipe vendors, weapon masters, the professions of trainers and vendors, flight points, boat and zeppelin routes and icon appearance. Clear saved spots is its last entry. It holds no notes and nothing that is not about the map.

## Changed

- None. The other windows behave as in 0.5.151.

## Fixed

- None.

## Limits

The places and the skills of the weapon masters come from the Warcraft Tavern guide for WoW Forever; only the place of Woo Ping was also seen in the game. Buliwyf and Sayoc stand at the coordinates of their shop. The weapons each class can use are Classic knowledge, not read from the client.

## Upgrading

Install over the old folders and restart the game fully once. Your saved data and settings are kept. The thirteen optional modules only carry the new version number: their catalogue data is unchanged.
