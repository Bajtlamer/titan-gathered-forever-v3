# Titan Gathered Forever: Auction house 0.6.0

This module is now the **Auction house** module (it was Auction items). Every auction feature of Titan Gathered Forever comes with it:

- the price scan of the auction house and auction prices in item tooltips
- the Sell tab undercut (Buyout price filled with the lowest buyout minus 2, 5, 10 or 15 percent)
- Market history beside the Sell tab
- the sales ledger read from the mailbox (`/tgf sales`) and the auction row of the Economy tab
- auction prices in the Crafting profit tab and the reagent window
- the Auction items catalogue, as before

Without this module the core keeps working with vendor prices and says where the auction features went. Your scanned prices and recorded sales are kept in the core's saved data, so enabling the module brings them back.

Requires the Titan Gathered Forever core 0.6.0. Titan Panel is optional. Install over the old folder and fully restart WoW.
